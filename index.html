RJ Love Shah’s journey · From CA to RJ

  1. 2011The beginning

    What began as an uncertain journey in 2011 turned into a story of passion, perseverance, and finding one’s true calling.

  2. 2015The first stage

    While pursuing Chartered Accountancy, Love discovered his love for the stage during a college cultural event, leading him to host his first official event in 2015.

  3. 2017Chartered Accountant

    True to his determination to finish what he started, he became a Chartered Accountant in July 2017.

  4. 2018On air · 94.3 MY FM

    But the stage had other plans. In 2018, he stepped into the world of radio as an RJ with 94.3 MY FM, bringing his voice to Ahmedabad.

  5. TodayEvent Host · Entertainer · Voice Artist

    RJ Love Shah has evolved into a seasoned Event Host, Entertainer, and Voice Artist, commanding stages across the country and transforming large-scale celebrations, weddings, and corporate events into unforgettable experiences.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I book RJ Love Shah for an event?

Send your event date, city and type through the form below, WhatsApp or email info@rjloveshah.com. Book in advance: wedding-season dates go first.

Which languages does he host in?

Hindi, English and Gujarati. He mixes them to match the crowd, which works well for Gujarati families, NRI guests and corporate audiences from across India and abroad.

Does he travel for events?

Yes. He hosts across India and internationally: from Reliance Navratri in Jamnagar and destination weddings in Udaipur to events in Sri Lanka and NRI weddings with guests from around the world.

What kind of events does he host?

Corporate shows (family days, team building, award nights, product launches), luxury weddings (carnival and haldi, sangeet, baraat, reception) and sports and live events such as Navratri, concerts and launches.

Which celebrities has he shared the stage with?

Vicky Kaushal, Kriti Sanon, Rashmika Mandanna, Disha Patani, Varun Dhawan, Malaika Arora, Tiger Shroff, Sidharth Malhotra, Mouni Roy, Taapsee Pannu, Neha Kakkar and Piyush Mishra, among others.

Is he really a Chartered Accountant?

Yes. Love is a qualified CA and was felicitated by WIRC of ICAI as a Chartered Accountant who also excels as an artist.
