Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I book RJ Love Shah for an event?

Send your event date, city and type through the form below, WhatsApp +91 73839 72757 or email rjloveshah12@gmail.com. Book in advance: wedding-season dates go first.

Which languages does he host in?

Hindi, English and Gujarati. He mixes them to match the crowd, which works well for Gujarati families, NRI guests and corporate audiences from across India and abroad.

Where is he based? Does he travel?

He is based in Ahmedabad and hosts across India and internationally: from Reliance Navratri in Jamnagar and destination weddings in Udaipur to events in Sri Lanka and NRI weddings with guests from around the world.

What kind of events does he host?

Corporate shows (family days, team building, award nights, product launches), luxury weddings (carnival and haldi, sangeet, baraat, reception) and sports and live events such as Navratri, concerts and launches.

Which celebrities has he shared the stage with?

Vicky Kaushal, Kriti Sanon, Rashmika Mandanna, Disha Patani, Varun Dhawan, Malaika Arora, Tiger Shroff, Sidharth Malhotra, Mouni Roy, Taapsee Pannu, Neha Kakkar and Piyush Mishra, among others.

Is he really a Chartered Accountant?

Yes. Love is a qualified CA and was felicitated by the Ahmedabad branch of WIRC of ICAI as a Chartered Accountant who also excels as an artist.
