Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does RJ Love Shah host destination weddings?

Yes. He is based in Ahmedabad, travels across India for weddings and is open to destination weddings abroad.

Has RJ Love Shah hosted NRI or international weddings?

Yes, including an Indo-Italian wedding. He hosts in English, Hindi and Gujarati so families and guests from abroad can follow every moment.

Can he host in Gujarati for family functions?

Yes. He hosts in Gujarati, Hindi and English and switches between them for mixed families.

How early should we book him for a wedding?

As early as possible. Wedding-season dates go first, so send your dates on WhatsApp to check availability.
